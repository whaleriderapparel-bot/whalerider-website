OUR STORY

FROM THE SAND
TO THE PLAINS.

Whale Rider started in 2009 hand-printing surf tees a few blocks from the Wilmington, North Carolina shoreline. Life carried us landlocked to Oklahoma, but the coastal passion never left.

Every tee, tail, and trucker cap carries the coast — salt in the veins, dirt on the boots.
