WHALE RIDER

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Whale Rider is an apparel brand based in Owasso, Oklahoma. This policy explains how personal information may be handled when you browse our website, explore our products, find upcoming pop-up events, or contact us. Our website is delivered through Cloudflare. The website currently operates as a product lookbook and event-announcement site and does not offer online checkout.

PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information you give us. If you contact us by email or social media, we may receive your name, contact details, and the information you choose to share.

Website technical information. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, device and browser information, timestamps, requested pages, and security-related data when providing website hosting, delivery, and protection. Additional analytics information may be collected if website analytics are enabled.

Pop-up purchases. Purchases made in person may be processed through separate payment or point-of-sale providers. Those providers' privacy policies describe their processing of payment information.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to respond to inquiries, communicate about Whale Rider products and events, maintain and protect the website, evaluate website performance, and comply with applicable law.

CLOUDFLARE HOSTING AND DATA PROCESSING

Whale Rider's website is hosted and delivered using Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare may process website traffic and security-related information to deliver and protect our site. This does not mean that Whale Rider stores customers' credit-card details in Cloudflare storage. The website does not currently accept online payments. For more about Cloudflare's practices, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

Cloudflare and other services may use cookies or similar technologies for site functionality, security, and performance. If we add optional analytics, advertising, or email-marketing tools, we will update this policy with relevant disclosures and choices.

SHARING OF INFORMATION

We may share information with providers supporting website delivery, security, communications, and other business services. We may disclose information where required by law, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to protect legal rights. Information you submit to external social-media or payment services is also subject to those services' own policies.

DATA SECURITY AND RETENTION

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or transmission is perfectly secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, customer service, dispute resolution, and legal obligations.

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about personal data we hold, or to object to certain uses. Contact us to submit a privacy request. We may verify your identity and will respond as required by applicable law.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES

Links to external websites, payment services, or social-media services are governed by those third parties' privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, service providers, or legal requirements. The revised version will appear here with an updated date.

CONTACT

For questions or privacy requests, email whaleriderapparel@gmail.com.
